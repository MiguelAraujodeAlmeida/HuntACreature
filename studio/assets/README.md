## Prompts usados

**Recursos:** “Create exactly THREE separate polished 3D toy icons, each centered in its own equal-width vertical third of a transparent square sprite sheet: a chunky golden coin with embossed spiral/loop, a bright cyan crystalline four-point XP star, and a coiled golden rope lasso with small metal hook. Strong silhouette at 40px, clean bevels, soft highlights, coherent cheerful adventure-game lighting, no text, no background, no external shadow.”

**Bolsa:** “A cheerful compact explorer's creature satchel, teal canvas body with warm leather flap, small golden rope loop clasp and tiny paw-shaped embossed mark. Three-quarter front view, premium toy-like 3D game icon, chunky silhouette legible at 52px, transparent background, no text.”
